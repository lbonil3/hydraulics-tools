# Crossing-view sprites

The ground, water, and road tiles in `assets/iso-atlas.png` are from Kenney's **Isometric Landscape** pack (also called Isometric Tiles Landscape), by Kenney Vleugels.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/isometric-tiles-landscape
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Credit is not required. The pack is included so the app works offline.

The atlas keeps only the tiles the crossing uses (grass, water, road) plus three recolored water frames for the wave cycle. Headwalls, wingwalls, barrel mouths, riprap, trees, cars, guardrail posts, and the outlet flow streaks were drawn for this app in the same flat isometric colors and 2:1 angle so they sit on that grid. Those pieces are not from the Kenney pack.
